Privacy Policy

AI Workday Assistant • Last updated: November 16, 2026

AI Workday Assistant is a browser extension designed to help you plan and organize your workday. This Privacy Policy explains what information the extension stores, how it is used, and when it may be shared.

1. Privacy at a Glance

  • Your tasks, schedules, and settings are stored locally in your browser.
  • The developer operates no servers and does not receive your data.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, or developer-managed accounts.
  • Information is sent to an AI provider only when you enable and use AI features.

2. Information Stored Locally

The extension uses your browser's extension storage to save the following information:

  • Tasks and notes: Titles, descriptions, notes, priorities, categories, estimates, dates, deadlines, dependencies, and completion status.
  • Schedule: Planned time blocks, manually entered meetings, breaks, and working hours.
  • Work records: Focus sessions and reminders.
  • Preferences: Working hours, time zone, reminder preferences, theme, and assistant name.
  • AI conversations: Your messages, AI responses, and proposed task or schedule changes.
  • AI credentials: API keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Google Gemini, or Anthropic Claude. ChatGPT sign-in tokens and account email may also be stored.

This information remains within the browser profile where the extension is installed. The extension does not independently synchronize it.

Credential security: Credentials are stored without encryption in the extension's private storage. Ordinary websites cannot directly read this storage, but someone with access to your browser profile may be able to. Avoid using the extension on devices you do not trust.

3. Information Shared with AI Providers

AI features are optional. Requests are made only after you connect a provider, enable its data-sharing consent option, and send a message or refresh the available model list.

When you use AI Chat, the following information may be sent directly from your browser to your selected provider:

  • Your message and up to six recent conversation messages.
  • A summary of up to 120 tasks, including titles, status, priority, categories, estimates, time spent, dates, deadlines, and dependencies.
  • Task descriptions and notes, only when the corresponding sharing option is enabled.
  • Working hours, working days, lunch and break times, and time zone.
  • Start and end times of meetings and scheduled blocks.
  • Meeting titles, unless disabled in Chat options.
  • Your chosen assistant name, if set.
  • Your API key or sign-in token for request authentication.

5. Information Received by the Developer

The developer does not collect, receive, or store your tasks, conversations, credentials, or other personal information.

The extension does not use developer-operated servers, analytics services, advertising systems, or tracking technologies.

6. Data Sharing and Sale

Your information is never sold or rented. It is shared only with your selected AI provider when you use AI functionality, as described in this policy.

Your information is not used by the extension for advertising, profiling, or credit-related decisions.

7. Browser Permissions

AI Workday Assistant requests only permissions required for its functionality:

  • Side panel: Displays the assistant within your browser's sidebar.
  • Alarms and notifications: Schedules reminders and workday check-ins.
  • Storage: Saves local information and ChatGPT sign-in credentials.
  • AI provider access: Allows direct communication with the supported AI provider endpoints.
  • Localhost access: Access to http://127.0.0.1 is used exclusively for the ChatGPT authentication redirect.

During ChatGPT sign-in, the authentication process redirects to a local address. The extension reads the authorization code from that redirect.

This permission is not used to communicate with unrelated applications or services on your computer.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your locally stored information remains available until you delete it.

You can:

  • Delete individual tasks, meetings, and AI conversations.
  • Remove saved API credentials using Forget key.
  • Disconnect ChatGPT using Sign out, which removes the associated account information and conversation from the extension.
  • Remove all locally stored information through Settings → Delete all local data.
  • Export tasks and work records through Settings → Export local data. Exported files exclude credentials and AI conversations.

Uninstalling the extension or clearing its browser storage removes locally stored information.

Uninstalling does not terminate sessions maintained by OpenAI. Sign out before uninstalling if you want to disconnect your ChatGPT account.

To request deletion of information retained by an AI provider, contact that provider directly.

9. Children's Privacy

AI Workday Assistant is not intended for children under the age of 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
